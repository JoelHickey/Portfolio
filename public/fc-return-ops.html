Core Strategy

PRINCIPLES

Positioning

  • Problem + proof, never title + ask
  • "Here's what I've been building" not "Would there be a role?"
  • Sell a capability they don't have, not an old job + raise
  • Agentic layer on top of senior PD fundamentals

Channel Tactics

  • Slack: weekly substantive posts, not link dumps
  • Share takes, screenshots, before/after, specific numbers
  • Goal: be seen doing this, not following AI news
  • Low-pressure stage → natural intro to bigger conversation

De-Risking

  • Offer 2-3 month embed if FT is hard sell
  • One team → measurable results → FT conversation
  • De-risks for them, supports higher comp after proof
  • Trial lets you demonstrate before they commit
