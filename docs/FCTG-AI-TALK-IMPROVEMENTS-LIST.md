# FCTG AI Talk — Improvement List (Rob’s feedback)

**Context:** 20 designers on FCTG’s Global Design Team. Build on the existing setup at https://www.2097.io/ (AI Talk — FCTG Design Team). Goal: turn it into an energising, practical session for a creative group of 20.

---

## 1. Lean into the low adoption stat as the “invigoration” hook

- **2026 data:** Only 31% of designers use AI for core creative work (vs 59% of developers).
- **Framing:** “We’re the early majority who get to shape how AI works for travel design at FCTG.”
- **Action:** Add a simple **“State of Design 2026”** slide right after the agenda. Makes the talk feel current and empowering.

---

## 2. Translate the three pillars into designer language

Keep **Invigoration / Innovation / Impact**, but make every example feel like it was made for a travel design studio.

| Pillar      | Current feel (if still generic) | Designer-specific upgrade (recommended) |
|------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Invigoration** | Re-energising creativity | Beat creative block in <60 seconds, generate 50 holiday moodboard variations while you grab coffee, reclaim 4–6 hours/week from repetitive tasks |
| **Innovation** | New tools & processes | Figma AI + Firefly + Midjourney v7 workflows for travel campaigns, AI style-consistent hero images, auto-responsive layouts, personalised itinerary visuals at scale |
| **Impact** | Measurable outcomes | Real travel examples: 2–3× faster campaign asset delivery, higher A/B test winners, 12% salary uplift for AI-savvy designers, direct lifts in booking conversions from better visuals |

---

## 3. Make it a hands-on workshop (for ~20 people)

- Turn **15–20 minutes** into live group activities:
  - Everyone opens Figma/Midjourney together.
  - **Prompt battle:** “Create a Summer 2026 Escape campaign hero in 90 seconds using the FCTG brand palette.”
  - Share screen + group critique.
  - End with **“Your personal 7-day AI win”** — each designer leaves with one new habit.
- Shifts from “talk” to “we all level-up together.”

---

## 4. Add the right 2026 tool stack for travel designers

**Dedicated slide or section:**

- **Daily drivers:** Figma AI (auto-layout, component suggestions), Adobe Firefly (safe commercial generative fill + brand-safe training), Midjourney/Recraft (high-aesthetic travel concepts).
- **Travel-specific:** Prompt templates, e.g. “FCTG hero image, vibrant Australian beach escape, consistent brand red #BF0010, 16:9, photorealistic yet illustrative.”
- **Workflow upgrade:** From brief → 30 concepts → refined in Figma in under 30 min.

## 5. Visual & structural polish

- More **before/after carousels** (generic stock → FCTG-branded travel visual).
- Heavy use of **FCTG red accents** + subtle globe/contrail background (keep existing style).
- **Shorten to ~35–40 slides max** — give designers breathing room.
- One **“Responsible AI for Creatives”** slide (copyright, brand consistency, when to keep it 100% human).
