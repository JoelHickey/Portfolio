# Activity comparison: current vs suggested (improvements list)

---

## Your current activity

**Slides:** Level up to agentic (slide 40) + Rounds (slide 41)

**Format:** ~55 min total (15 + 25 + 15), pairs, structured rounds.

**Problem:** Moon trip booking flow — “How might we create a Moon trip booking flow that’s easy and enjoyable for customers to use?”

**Setup:** Pairs. **Miro** for collaboration; **Claude Code** for the agent.

**Process:** Start with vibe (aim to iterate 2–3 times), then level up to agentic. Write clear prompts. Redirect the agent when it drifts.

**Rounds:**

| Round   | Time   | Focus        | What they do |
|--------|--------|--------------|--------------|
| 1      | ~15 min| Vibe (warm-up) | Explore flows and tone. “How would you approach designing a booking flow for a Moon trip?” Chat, iterate, exploratory. |
| 2      | ~25 min| Agentic (main) | Give the agent a clear mission. “Create a 3-step Moon booking flow: date picker, cabin selection (economy, business, first), add-ons and terms. Include copy and layout.” Multi-step, delegated. |
| 3      | ~15 min| Show & tell  | Share clearest Moon booking outputs. Vote on which flow we’d ship. Debrief: How could this apply to our Earth bookings? |

**Outputs:** Reusable prompts, agent responses, generated UI/copy.

**Outcomes:** Improved prompting skills, comfort with quick iteration, ability to delegate to the agent with confidence.

**Tools:** Miro, Claude (text/code agent). No Figma, no image-gen (Midjourney/Firefly).

**Brand:** Generic “Moon trip” — no FCTG palette or campaign brief.

**Close:** Show & tell + debrief. No explicit “7-day AI win” or personal habit.

## Suggested activity (from improvements list)

**Format:** 15–20 minutes total, live group.

**What they do:**

- Everyone opens **Figma / Midjourney** together.
- **Prompt battle:** “Create a Summer 2026 Escape campaign hero in 90 seconds using the FCTG brand palette.”
- **Share screen + group critique.**
- End with **“Your personal 7-day AI win”** — each designer leaves with one new habit.

**Goal:** Shift from “talk” to “we all level-up together.”

**Tools:** Figma, Midjourney (visual/design tools).

**Brand:** FCTG — Summer 2026 Escape, FCTG brand palette.

**Close:** Explicit “7-day AI win” / one new habit per person.
