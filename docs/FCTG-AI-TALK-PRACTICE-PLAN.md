# FCTG AI Talk — Practice plan for the day

A structured plan so you can rehearse and execute confidently.

---

## 1. Know the deck (content + flow)

**Goal:** You can say what each section is for and the one thing you want people to remember from it.

| Block | Slides (approx) | Your one-line intent |
|-------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Opener** | 1–2 | Set the stakes: invigoration, innovation, impact; empathy as the human edge. |
| **Context** | 3–5 | What we’ll cover; State of Design (designers vs devs); looking back to look ahead. |
| **Craft / fundamentals** | 6–8 | Questioning fundamentals; design process (Discover → Deliver). |
| **Wider env** | 9 | Bottlenecks, who owns them. |
| **Monumental moments** | 10–16 | Energy, Strength, Speed, Iteration, Imagination, Calmness, Mystical Code, Empowerment. |
| **Practices** | 17–18 | Getting started. |
| **Technical** | 19–28 | Model vs agent; architectures; vibe vs agentic; prompting; context; intervention. |
| **Productivity** | 29–31 | Agentic & designer productivity; **Then vs Now** (you steer, agent executes). |
| **Tech / pipeline** | 32–35 | Tech stack, Pipeline, Cursor, GitHub, Vercel. |
| **Design systems + testing** | 36–39 | Design systems, design system in practice, Testing, Helpful tips. |
| **Activity** | 40–42 | Level up to agentic (problem, setup, process, outputs); Rounds (1, 2, 3). |
| **Close** | 43–45 | Three pillars; What we hope you take away; Opportunity; Thank you. |

**Practice:**  
- Run through the deck once with **only the titles** visible (or a printed list). For each slide, say out loud: “This slide is about … and I want them to remember ….”  
- Then do one full click-through while **narrating in your own words** (no reading bullet-for-bullet). Aim for 2–3 minutes per “block” so you feel the pace.

---

## 2. Rehearse the talk (timing + transitions)

**Goal:** Hit your target length and land key messages without rushing.

- **Decide total time** (e.g. 45 min talk + 10 min buffer + activity, or 60 min total).  
- **Assign time per block** (e.g. Opener 3 min, Technical 15 min, Then vs Now 5 min, Activity 55 min, Close 5 min).  
- **Mark “must not skip” slides:** e.g. State of Design, Then vs Now, Model or agent?, Vibe vs agentic, Level up (activity), What we hope you take away, Opportunity.  
- **Rehearse at least twice:**  
  - **Run 1:** Full run with a timer. Note where you go over or under.  
  - **Run 2:** Same again; tighten long bits, add a phrase where you were lost.  
- **Transitions:** Write one short bridge for each major turn (e.g. “So we’ve seen the process — now what does that look like in practice? This is Then vs Now.”). Say them out loud until they feel natural.

**Practice:**  
- Do one **dry run with a friend or colleague** (even 20 min). Ask: “Where did I lose you? Where did I rush?”  
- Optional: **Record yourself** on the Then vs Now and Activity slides; watch back for clarity and pace.

---

## 3. Practise the activity (facilitation)

**Goal:** You can set up the activity, keep time, and debrief without reading from the slide.

**Before you practise:**

- [ ] Miro board: same structure as `docs/miro-activity-board.md` (Problem, Round 1, Round 2, Show & tell, optional Cheat sheet).  
- [ ] Voting enabled in the Show & tell frame before Round 3.  
- [ ] Test the Miro link and Claude (or chosen tool) link; have them open in tabs.

**Rehearse out loud:**

1. **Intro (1–2 min)**  
   “We’re going to work in pairs. You’ll use [Miro] to collaborate and [Claude Code] as the agent. The problem is: How might we create a Moon trip booking flow that’s easy and enjoyable for customers? We’re not building a real product — we’re practising how you work with an agent: vibe first, then agentic.”

2. **Round 1 (~15 min)**  
   “Round 1 is vibe — about 15 minutes. Explore flows and tone. Try something like: ‘How would you approach designing a booking flow for a Moon trip?’ Chat, iterate, go wild. Paste your prompts and outputs into the Round 1 area on Miro.”

3. **Round 2 (~25 min)**  
   “Round 2 is agentic — about 25 minutes. Give the agent a clear mission. For example: ‘Create a 3-step Moon booking flow: date picker, cabin selection, add-ons and terms. Include copy and layout.’ One clear prompt; let it run; you review and refine. Paste those prompts and outputs in the Round 2 area.”

4. **Round 3 (~15 min)**  
   “Round 3 is show & tell. Put your best Moon booking output in the Show & tell area. We’ll vote on which flow we’d ship. Then we’ll debrief: How could this apply to our Earth bookings?”

**Practice:**  
- Say the above **without looking** at the slide. Time yourself.  
- If you have a colleague, do a **mini run** (e.g. 5 min vibe, 10 min agentic, 5 min show & tell) so you feel the rhythm.

## 4. Q&A readiness

**Goal:** You can answer the likeliest questions in 1–2 sentences without flipping through notes.

- Use **`docs/FCTG-AI-TALK-Q-and-A.md`**.  
- **Pick 10 questions** you think you’ll get (e.g. “How did you measure Then vs Now?”, “Why Moon not a real flow?”, “Isn’t this for engineers?”, “What’s the one thing to do in 7 days?”).  
- For each, **say the answer out loud** in under 30 seconds. If you ramble, shorten the answer and try again.  
- **Practise 3 curveballs:** e.g. “What would you remove from the deck?”, “What’s the biggest mistake you’ve made with AI?”, “How would you explain agentic to a non-technical stakeholder?”

**Practice:**  
- Get someone to **ask you 5 random Q&A questions**; answer without reading.  
- If you blank, note the question and add a one-line fallback (“I’d need to double-check that — happy to follow up after.”).

---

## 5. Week‑before checklist

- [ ] **Deck:** Full run-through with timer; adjust script/transitions.  
- [ ] **Activity:** Say the activity intro + rounds out loud; confirm Miro and tool link.  
- [ ] **Q&A:** Run through your chosen 10 questions + 3 curveballs out loud.  
- [ ] **Tech:** Laptop, dongles, backup (e.g. PDF export or link).  
- [ ] **Room:** Know how you’ll share screen and whether you need to move for the activity.

---

## 6. Day‑before

- [ ] **One short rehearsal:** 15–20 min: opener → Then vs Now → Activity intro → Close.  
- [ ] **Miro:** Board ready; voting on for Show & tell.  
- [ ] **Links:** Miro and Claude (or alternative) open in a browser; test once.  
- [ ] **Q&A:** Skim the Q&A doc once.  
- [ ] **Sleep:** Prioritise rest so you’re sharp.

---

## 7. On the day

- [ ] **Arrive early:** Test screen share, audio, and one click through the first 5 slides.  
- [ ] **Water + backup:** Have water; know where your backup deck is.  
- [ ] **Activity:** Before you start the activity, remind people to open Miro and the agent; give them 30 seconds to get there.  
- [ ] **Time:** Keep an eye on the clock for Rounds 1–3; give a 2‑minute warning before each round ends.  
- [ ] **Debrief:** After voting, ask: “How could this apply to our Earth bookings?” and take 2–3 answers before wrapping.

## Suggested schedule (example)

| When | What |
|------|------|
| **1 week before** | Know-the-deck pass; first full timed run; note long/short sections. |
| **5–6 days before** | Second full run; fix timing; write transitions; practise activity script. |
| **3–4 days before** | Q&A drill (10 questions + 3 curveballs); mini activity run if possible. |
| **1–2 days before** | Short rehearsal (opener → Then vs Now → activity → close); Miro + links check. |
| **Day before** | 15–20 min rehearsal; final Miro/link check; skim Q&A; rest. |
| **On the day** | Tech check; water + backup; time the rounds; debrief with one clear question. |
