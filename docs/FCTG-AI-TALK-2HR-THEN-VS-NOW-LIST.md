## Response summary: hybrid approach

- **Yes** — a personal “Then vs Now” UI workflow story should be a **highlight** of the session.
- Don’t **replace** the group exercise; **swap or shorten** it, or **run both**.
- Personal story = credibility and emotional pull; group exercise = hands-on “do, not just watch.”

---

## Why the personal story works well

- Designers connect with **real war stories** from a peer in the same company/role.
- Anchors **Invigoration** — e.g. “I used to spend 4 hours on one hero asset; now I get 30 concepts in 15 minutes.”
- Can be a **live screen-share demo (5–8 min)**:
  - **Old workflow:** manual moodboarding, endless iterations, Figma only.
  - **New AI workflow:** prompt → Firefly/Recraft concepts → Figma refine → brand-safe export.
- Builds **trust** before asking them to try it themselves.

---

## Why keep (or adapt) the group exercise

- 20 creatives in a room = they want to **do**, not just watch.
- “Trip to the moon” is fun; tying it to **travel** is stronger — e.g. “Design the ultimate 2027 FCTG ‘Escape to the Stars’ campaign.”
- Best practice (NN/g, 2025–26): **model it first** (your story), then **immediate hands-on practice**.

---

## Recommended 2-hour structure (with coffee break)

**Total: 120 minutes**

| Time        | Section |
|------------|---------|
| 0–10 min   | Welcome + Agenda (Slide 2) |
| 10–45 min  | **Invigoration** — Your personal UI Then-vs-Now story + live demo (35 min) |
| 45–60 min  | Innovation quick wins & tool stack (15 min) |
| 60–75 min  | **Coffee break** (15 min) |
| 75–105 min | Hands-on group exercise (adapted “Trip to the Moon” → 25 min create + 5 min share-out) |
| 105–115 min | Impact & real FCTG/travel metrics |
| 115–120 min | Your 7-day action plan + Q&A |

Goal: energy high, breathing room, everyone leaves with a tangible win.

## Quick tips to make it land

1. **Personal story:** Max **8 minutes** — script it: 2 min old pain, 4 min live demo, 2 min “what changed for me.”
2. **Group exercise:** Give everyone the **same FCTG brand kit + 3 prompt templates** so results are on-brand and shareable.
3. **Record** the Then-vs-Now demo as a short **Loom** in case someone joins late.
